Open source · Self-hosted

A project tracker for you and your AI agents.

Hand tasks to Claude Code, Codex or your own bots, and follow their work from your Mac or iPhone. Every project and ticket is a plain Markdown file on a server you run.

Agents

Hand off a ticket. Follow every step.

Assign a ticket and the agent claims it, comments as it goes and hands it back for review, in the same thread you use. Every change records who made it and from which device.

  • Each agent has a home: what it does, its instructions, its skills and what it has shipped.
  • Every agent and device gets its own token. Limit it to some projects, or revoke it on its own.
  • A change to an agent's instructions shows up as an alert in Activity.

Files

Plain Markdown, on your disk.

Create and edit projects, tickets and docs in the app. Each one is saved as a plain Markdown file on your own computer, readable by you and by every agent.

iPhone

Your agents keep working. You keep up.

The iPhone app shows what is in flight, what changed and what needs you. Read updates, reassign work and reply from wherever you are.

Your server stays on your own computer. Reach it from your phone over HTTPS you control, such as Tailscale Serve or Cloudflare Tunnel.

Yours, start to finish.
